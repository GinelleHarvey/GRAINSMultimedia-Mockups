# THE 386 EXCHANGE — Website Mockup

**Connecting people, resources, and opportunities.**

The 386 Exchange is a proposed community platform rooted in the 386 area, with a vision to connect people, resources, and opportunities across Florida and beyond.

This website is a demonstration mockup created by GRAINSMultimedia LLC. 

The platform name is a proposed concept name, and all sample names, listings, organizations, contact details, payment options, and services shown are fictional placeholders for demonstration purposes only.

No real donations, payments, sales, services, or commitments are offered, accepted, or processed through this mockup.
